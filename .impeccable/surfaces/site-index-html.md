Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS on Cloudflare Pages, reduced motion honoured, no phone number published. ## Direction contract

THESIS: A cinematic dark portfolio where the analyst looks at you and you look back through his lens. The page behaves like analysis: the portrait is read through a cursor lens, skills stack into a live bar chart, case studies carry their own real numbers drawn as charts. Refuses the light-grey CV-with-charts analyst template.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black ground, cream type, one signal red used as stage light (radial washes) and for live data marks; deep-red shadow fields. Bricolage Grotesque display, Instrument Serif italic for one accent word per heading, Archivo body, Martian Mono for data labels. Pill controls, hairline rules, a floating pill nav.

STORY: A brief intro runs "the query" on Ravidu, then the hero shows who he is. The approach line lights up as you read; about gives four proven figures; skills fall into a chart by suit of work; five case studies scroll sideways with real figures; a timeline with a growing red line; a big email close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed red-lit portrait on the right two-thirds; headline left at display scale with one italic accent word; one-line lede; Email me (cream pill) and See the work; a hint pill inviting the cursor to analyse the photo; a circular lens follows the cursor showing the true-colour photo with a grid and readout.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). "Scratch the whole idea make something similar to the website that I gave use new ideas" (reference: hameduiux.online, "something I really like can you make it darker please"). Similar register and structure; the signature effects are new (analyst lens, chart-stacking skills, data-drawn case studies, query intro).
